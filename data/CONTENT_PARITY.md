## What this means

A row at 100% means every item in that framework carries that
audit-grade field. A row at 0% means the framework is
catalog-only on that dimension - the gap-assessment UI falls
back to the catalog summary, which works but doesn't carry the
same consultant insight per item.

Pre-existing scaffolding for NIST CSF lives in
`data/csf-analyst-content.js` (engagement-scoped explainers,
analyst questions, evidence prompts, self-check prompts, learn
docs). That fills the "workshop facilitation" gap but not the
per-subcategory audit-grade gap above.
